1. Your Mission: Do 95% Autonomously, Then Guide the Human

Core operating rule for AI agents: Never dump a raw first assembly onto the user and hope they figure out what's wrong. Run l2d_tools.py validate, polish_audit.py --prompt, and qa_runner.py yourself, inspect every zoomed close-up image directly with vision, fix every structural seam, partial-mask tubular openings, build the 3D side-of-head stack, and reach FAIL=0 / 100 A+ before handing back a preview. Then proactively ask the user to inspect specific high-leverage spots — and tell them you will fix whatever they spot.

2. You Can Copy & Adapt Layers from Other Models in the Repo

Important hint: Do not reinvent every layer, expression piece, rig config, or shader pattern from scratch! If you have access to other .l2d bundles, unpacked models/*/viewer/ rigs, rigkit/ scripts, or layered .psd avatars in the workspace or repository (such as MIT-licensed nori.l2d, test-signal.l2d, starter.l2d, or any other avatar in the user's repo), inspect them and copy/adapt proven layers and rig patterns directly.

Unpack any reference model in seconds to inspect its exact textures, draw stack, masks, and motion physics (you can even fetch Nori directly from layer2d.com/player/nori.l2d or test her live in Player):

What to Copy / Adapt Directly

Eye sparkle highlights (E04/E04b), cheek blush gradients, forehead gloom/mood shades, flipbook FX atlases (sparkles, sweatdrops, tears, hearts), AEIOU vowel mouth templates (M04–M08), and clean blink/expression piece definitions — simply tint/scale to match your target character.

Anatomical & Garment Blueprints

Study working bare shoulder slopes (B02/B03), neck-to-chin tapers (B01), hand/wrist sprites (G01), flared open-arch sock insteps (S01), and partial-masked <ID>_back + <ID> cuff/sock pairs from existing repo models to match proven proportions and anchor geometry.

Copy Working Rig & Physics Presets

Reuse proven pack.json settings directly: Test Signal's solid non-rubbery motion block (bodyFollowLR: [0.025, 0.025], limit.bend: 0.25, no turnChain), pitchGroups, expr.blink object format, rigid pack.tail config, and face3d.py 3D side-of-head recipes.

Respect Ownership & Match Style

Reuse freely from MIT-licensed repo models (nori.l2d, test-signal.l2d, starter.l2d) or the user's own local models in the workspace. Always hue-match, line-weight-match, and scale copied layers so they blend invisibly into the target avatar's art style.

3. High-Impact Rigging Hints (Do All of These Without Being Asked)

  • Solve every limb and garment from real opaque end-anchors (never bounding-box centers). Cut sprites for arms, sleeves, hands, socks, and shoes often have slanted or off-center openings (e.g., wrist at 0.73w, shoe opening at 0.42w). Measure true opaque top/bottom centerlines and cross-section widths, then solve (cx, cy, w, h, rot) analytically so parent exit and child entry land within < 2 px with matching widths and zero dark cut-border seam lines.
  • Never crossfade hair (op = 1.0 always). Alpha-fading hair looks ghostly on turns. Stack solid physical depth shells — back (H01), crown fill behind animal ears (H02c), side-of-skull curtains (H02s/H04s), under-bangs (H03u), cheek framing locks (H04), and front bangs (H03C/R/L) — with signed horizontal parallax.
  • Never flip binary behind near center yaw (a = 0) over the forehead/brows. Lock depth shells (H02c/H03u/H02s/H04s) permanently to behind = 1.0 across every yaw angle, keep H03u outside the eyebrow X-band, and delay far-side H04 flips until |u| >= 0.28.
  • Protect kemonomimi / animal ear fluff & pad warped quads generously. Draw crown fill (H02c) behind animal ears with its top below ear roots, set pitchGroups[earId] = "ear", and keep pad = 320–420 px so warped tips never clip.
  • Remember inverse warp grid D sign convention. The shader samples g_tex = g_screen + D(g_screen). To move rendered pixels visually by +Δg, subtract Δg from D.
  • Rigid pelvis rotation for fluffy tails — never wavy spring chains. Use pack.tail = {"id": "T01", "pivot": [px, py], "amp": 0.165, "freq": 2.45, "happyFaces": ["Happy", "Starry"]} with zero per-frame non-uniform sx/sy stretch.
  • Keep body motion crisp & non-rubbery. Omit motion.turnChain on fitted jackets, belts, or skirts (it squishes structured clothes when the head turns). Use bodyFollowLR: [0.025, 0.025], bodyFollow: [0.12, 0.25], limit.bend: 0.25, and wide bodyR.
  • Always wire clipTarget: true, expr.blink object & body.elbowHalf. Every mask parent (F01, E01R/L) needs clipTarget: true. expr.blink MUST be an object {"hide":[], "add":[], "drop":[], "squash": true} (never an array). Include bare arms, sleeves, AND *_back cuff layers in body.elbowHalf and outfits[...].hide.
  • Check open-arch shoes (Mary Janes, heels, strapped shoes) on bright green. Never leave narrow sock ankles ending inside an open shoe arch or dark sock outlines cutting across the arch. Smoothly flare the lower sock instep (y >= 0.66h) using scipy.ndimage.map_coordinates (never binary dilation!) so shaded sock fabric fills the arch wall-to-wall behind both shoe walls and the strap.

4. Automatic Partial Masking Protocol (Do This Pass Yourself!)

Whenever a 3D cylindrical garment or accessory wraps around an entering limb or neck — socks, sleeve cuffs, short sleeves, collars, chokers, wristbands, bracelets, leg warmers, belts, ribbons, boot tops, or open shoe rims — drawing the single flat cutout on top of skin leaves an ugly hollow back-rim ellipse floating in front of the arm, leg, or neck like a flat sticker.

How to split & partial-mask automatically:

  1. Detect the curved front-lip parabola across the opening of the garment/cuff sprite (where the front edge passes in front of the skin).
  2. Split the sprite into two pixel-locked RGBA layers sharing identical canvas size and affine placement:
    • <ID>_back (everything above/behind the front lip — the hollow dark interior & back rim) placed BEHIND the entering limb/neck in pack.layers[].
    • <ID> (the crisp front lip and body below it) placed IN FRONT OF the entering limb/neck.
  3. Register <ID>_back in the same outfits[...].hide toggle list AND the same body.elbowHalf / chains / kind bucket as <ID> so toggling jackets or bending elbows never leaves a detached ghost rim behind!

Even after your first pass: always explicitly ask the user if any other accessory, sleeve, collar, strap, or sock still needs partial masking — and reassure them you will split and mask it immediately.

5. Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist & Garment Drape Checklist

Seam / AreaWhat Goes WrongExact Autonomous Fix
Bare shoulders (Jacket OFF) 20–30px V-notch between B02 and B03R/L, translucent white alpha holes on shoulder ridge/armpit, or dark socket ink strokes. Render with hair AND outer jacket hidden. Keep outer shoulder crest & armpit 100% opaque (alpha=255), seat B03R/L tangent to B02's collarbone slope (0px V-notch), strip only internal overlapping ink, and feather strictly inside B02's opaque chest.
Sweater / cardigan shoulders (Jacket ON) Puffy floating shoulder peaks hovering above the bare shoulder slope, or a stray chest flap/tab sticking out beside the collar trim. Flatten high inner collar peaks on shoulder panels (J03/J04), lower outer sleeve caps onto B02's bare shoulder slope so zero air gap hovers above the collarbone, and trim protruding upper-inner chest tabs cleanly behind the collar edge.
Elbow seams (ON & OFF) Bare forearm (B04R/L) detaches from upper arm (B03R/L) when toggling a jacket OFF, or visible cut-border ink slices across the elbow. Anchor-solve B03 → B04 opaque endpoints within < 2px with matched widths, strip cut-edge ink along the joint overlap, and map BOTH bare arms and jacket sleeves in body.elbowHalf.
Wrist seams & sleeve cuffs Hands (G01R/L) float off cuffs, wrist anchor differs between jacket ON vs OFF, or uncut hollow cuff back rim draws over the wrist. Unify hand wrist centerlines across bare forearms and sleeved cuffs, split cuffs into J05_back (behind wrist) + J05 (front lip in front), and verify with a close-up wrist crop in both outfit states.
Camisole straps & chokers Choker hangs wide into empty air; camisole straps stop short, shoot too high, or aim at the choker when they should drape over the shoulders. Size choker A03 to 1.00–1.10× neck width centered on B01. Inspect concept art carefully: drape camisole/tank straps cleanly over the inner shoulders/trapezius (below the choker) unless it is explicitly a neck-tied halter!

6. Face, Eyes, 3D Side-of-Head & Neck Polish

Stem-Free, Ear-Free Face (F01)

Inspect F01 alone on bright green. Flood-fill interior face skin and strip BOTH any hanging pink neck stem below the chin (which swings off B01 on turns) AND any baked-on human ears on F01's sides so only separate F05/F06 ears remain.

Crease-Free Neck Cylinder (B01)

Trim flared upper jaw stubs off B01 down to slender mid-neck width AND paint out any baked U-shaped front-chin outline/fold on the upper neck interior (y = 0..120px) so no weird horizontal crease appears when the head turns!

Eyes, Tall Irises & Sculpted Lashes

Keep irises/pupils (E02/E03) centered slightly inward (outward_div <= 0%, never wall-eyed), sleek wide almond sclera (E01), slightly vertical-oval irises (e.g. ~1.3× taller than wide when matching the anime style), unified key-light E04 sparkles, and curved E06 lower lashes wrapped tightly along E01's rim.

Full 3D Side-of-Head Stack (face3d.py)

Calibrate a strictly convex ~38° turned jawline on F01 (zero side-chin dent!), add clipped rear-to-front cheek shading (F02tR/L) right before bounded monotonic side-jaw planes (F01sL/R, jaw_depth_px=14–20, max|D| <= 0.24, zero mid-yaw double chin), sculpted 3/4 nose (F03tR/L as front F03 → 0), and lush multi-strand side hair (H02s/H04s) behind H04.

7. Proactively Prompt the User for Visual Polish (Copy-Paste Handoff)

Even after your autonomous build hits 100.0/100 (A+) with 0 errors in polish_audit.py and qa_runner.py, subtle art-direction details benefit hugely from 1–3 fast human glances. Never end with just "Done!" — render clean close-ups (jacket ON, jacket OFF, front face, and 3/4 turns at a = -1.0, -0.5, +1.0) and send a structured check-in like the template below so the user knows exactly where to look and knows you'll fix anything they spot:

I've built and verified <Avatar>.l2d (l2d_tools validate: OK, polish_audit: 100/100 A+, qa_runner: 0 errors, OBS alpha=0).

Before we call it final, please take a quick look at these specific spots — point out anything that looks off and I'll fix it right away:

1. **Shoulder & elbow seams (jacket/outerwear ON and OFF):** Do the outer shoulders sit flat on the collarbone with no protruding flaps, and do the bare shoulders and elbows join seamlessly when the outer layer is toggled OFF?
2. **Wrist seams & hands:** Do the hands tuck cleanly into the sleeve cuffs / bare wrists with natural width and zero floating gap?
3. **Parts that need partial masking:** Check socks, sleeve cuffs, collars, chokers, straps, wristbands, belts, and shoe openings — if any edge looks like a flat sticker sitting on top of skin (instead of wrapping behind and in front of the limb), let me know and **I will split and partial-mask it for you**.
4. **3/4 head turns, chin & neck (`a = ±0.5, ±1.0`):** Does the side-of-head silhouette look smooth with no dent in the chin, no weird crease/fold on the neck, and full side hair?
5. **Face, eyes & strap drape:** Check eye shape, iris proportions/gaze focus, eyelashes, ears, and whether any straps drape over the right part of the shoulders vs neck.
6. **Shoes, accessories & motion:** Check open-arch shoes (wall-to-wall sock fill), choker/belt snugness, and body motion while turning.

Tell me any tweaks you want — even tiny ones — and I'll iterate until it's spotless!
